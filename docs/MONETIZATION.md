# Monetization Strategy

## Revenue Streams

SERVICE HUB SRI LANKA employs a multi-channel monetization approach to maximize revenue while providing value to both customers and service providers.

## 1. Premium Subscriptions (Primary Revenue)

### Free Tier
**Price:** Free forever
**Target:** New providers testing the platform

**Features:**
- Basic profile listing
- Limited to 3 services
- Appears in search results
- Basic contact information
- Ads displayed on profile
- Standard support

**Limitations:**
- Lower search ranking
- No analytics
- No featured badge
- Limited visibility
- Profile ads present

### Professional Tier
**Price:** LKR 2,500/month or LKR 25,000/year (17% discount)
**Target:** Established service providers seeking growth

**Features:**
✅ Featured profile badge
✅ Unlimited services
✅ Photo gallery (up to 15 images)
✅ Basic analytics dashboard
✅ Higher search ranking (2x boost)
✅ Priority in service requests
✅ Monthly performance report
✅ Email support (24h response)
✅ Reduced profile ads

**Expected Conversion:** 15% of active providers

### Elite Tier
**Price:** LKR 5,000/month or LKR 50,000/year (17% discount)
**Target:** Top-tier providers and businesses

**Features:**
✅ All Professional features
✅ Verified badge
✅ Top search ranking (5x boost)
✅ Advanced analytics & lead tracking
✅ No ads on profile
✅ Premium placement in all listings
✅ Featured in homepage carousel
✅ Unlimited gallery images
✅ Priority customer support
✅ Personal account manager
✅ Custom profile URL
✅ Service request alerts (real-time)

**Expected Conversion:** 5% of active providers

## 3. Lead Generation (Performance-Based)

### Pay-Per-Lead Model
**Price:** LKR 100-500 per lead (category-dependent)
**Target:** Providers who prefer performance-based pricing

**How it Works:**
1. Customer submits service request
2. Matching providers receive notification
3. Provider pays fee to view contact details
4. Provider contacts customer directly

**Lead Quality:**
- Verified contact information
- Detailed requirements
- Budget information
- Urgency level
- Location specifics

**Lead Pricing by Category:**
| Category | Price per Lead |
|----------|---------------|
| Electrician | LKR 150 |
| Plumber | LKR 150 |
| Car Mechanic | LKR 200 |
| Home Tutor | LKR 300 |
| Web Developer | LKR 500 |
| Event Planner | LKR 400 |

### Lead Credits System
- Providers purchase credit bundles
- Bundle discounts available
- Credits expire in 12 months

**Bundle Options:**
- 10 credits: LKR 2,000 (LKR 200/lead)
- 25 credits: LKR 4,500 (LKR 180/lead - 10% off)
- 50 credits: LKR 8,000 (LKR 160/lead - 20% off)
- 100 credits: LKR 14,000 (LKR 140/lead - 30% off)
